COMPATIBILITY

More than graphics

Console, files, dialogs and basic audio are the first test surfaces. General Windows apps and games are future work.

CONTROL

Your tab. Your files.

A dedicated worker runs the program. Stop ends it immediately. Generated files are available to download.

Runtime diagnostics & current limits

This is a bootstrap runtime, not a full Wine port. PE32 x86 only; no x64, CRT, TLS, SEH, DLL loading, threads, GDI, OpenGL or DirectX yet. Unsupported instructions and imports stop with a diagnostic. The examples are native Windows PE binaries compiled from assembly, not Wasm source ports.
